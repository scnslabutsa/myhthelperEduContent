<!---title: How is tamoxifen taken<sup>3</sup>? --->
---

*	Tamoxifen comes in pill form or a liquid form.
*	You take one pill every day, if possible at the same time.
*	If you have the liquid form of Tamoxifen (i.e., Soltamox), follow the instructions from your doctor on how to take it.
*	It is taken for 5-10 years.

***Common tamoxifen brand names***
* Nolvadex
* Tomifen
* Tamofen
*	Soltamox (liquid form)
